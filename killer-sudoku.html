Killer Sudoku – add up the dotted groups

Tap an empty square, then tap a number. Each dotted group must add up to the small number in its corner.

How to play

Killer sudoku follows the usual sudoku rules: every row, column and box contains each number 1 to 9 once. There are no starting numbers. Instead, the squares are joined in dotted groups, and the small number in the corner of each group is the total of that group.

A number can be used only once in each group. Use Notes for pencil marks, Check to see wrong numbers in red, and Hint to fill in the selected square. Your progress is saved on this device.
