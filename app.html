Step-by-step guide to your TOMfit app

Walk

TOMfit promotes brisk walking – a moderate-intensity form of walking, typically around 5–6.5 km/h (3–4 mph). One big button starts your walk.

  • Start and pause your walk with one button
  • Walking time, brisk minutes, distance, steps and speed
  • Simple daily goals: 30 min, 2 km, 3,000 steps – change them in Settings
  • Charts for today, this week and the last 12 weeks
  • Brisk walking means 100 or more steps a minute
Picture 1 of 2

Brisk walking is a moderate-intensity form of walking. The WHO recommends 150–300 minutes a week. Keep an eye on your pace: 100+ steps a minute, or about 5 km/h. TOMfit starts you at the easier end.

Health

Keep your important numbers in one place and see at a glance whether you are going in the right direction.

  • Record your blood glucose
  • Record your blood pressure and pulse
  • Record your weight – BMI is worked out for you
  • Log your fasting time between last and first meal
  • A health report to show your doctor
Picture 1 of 2

I check my blood glucose and blood pressure at least once a day, I keep an eye on my weight and BMI, and I like to check the time between my last and first meal – all to be sure I am going in the right direction.

Medication & supplements

Your full list in one place, with the times you take them and a simple tick when it’s done.

  • Add a medication or supplement: name, dose and form
  • Up to 5 daily times, e.g. breakfast, lunch, bed time
  • Today’s checklist – tap “mark taken”
  • Choose days for items you don’t take every day
  • A printable list for your doctor or pharmacist
Picture 1 of 2

It is not easy to remember all your medications and supplements. Your doctor or pharmacist asks “What do you take?” – and your mind goes blank. I know, because I had that problem too – but not any more. Register all your medications and supplements in the TOMfit app and have the full list in your pocket, ready when you need it.

Exercises

Walking is a great start, but muscles need a little more. TOMfit has 10 easy exercises with large, clear pictures.

  • Daily core: chair squats, calf raises, wall push-ups and more
  • Kettlebell exercises twice a week
  • Count your reps against your own starting point
  • A safety page before you begin
  • Go at your own pace – no levels, no pressure
Picture 1 of 2

The TOMfit app invites you to choose at least 3 very basic daily core exercises – 10 minutes at most – and at least 3 kettlebell exercises, 2–3 times a week. Read the safety notes and tips before you start. Charts show your progress.

Settings

Make TOMfit yours. Keep only the parts you need and set goals that suit you.

  • Switch off the parts you don’t need
  • Set your walking goals
  • Units set automatically – metric or imperial
  • Choose your kettlebell weight
  • All your data stays on your phone – back it up any time
Picture 1 of 1

The TOMfit app is very flexible – it is designed to work for you and with you. Adjust your baseline goals, switch off any part of the app you don’t use, set your kettlebell weight, and more.
