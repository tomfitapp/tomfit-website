Sarcopenia: stop and reverse muscle loss

After 50, muscle and strength slowly fade unless we use them. This guide explains why muscle matters for balance, bones, blood sugar and memory, gives a simple 30-second home self-check, and shows how strength exercise and protein can stop and even reverse the loss.

In short
  1. Muscle protects you from falls and fractures
  2. Strength exercise 2–3 times a week is the key
  3. Spread protein across every meal

Intermittent fasting – a practical guide

Gentle daily fasting windows such as 12:12 or 14:10 can help blood sugar, waistline and energy after 50. The guide compares the schedules, explains what changes for men and women, and gives a simple plan to start, keep going and break a fast gently.

In short
  1. Start gently – 12:12 before 14:10 or 16:8
  2. Protect your muscles with protein at every meal
  3. Who should not fast, and when to ask your doctor

Walking at 100 or more steps a minute turns an ordinary walk into real exercise for your heart, blood sugar and brain – without stressing your joints. Includes an easy weekly goal and a music playlist to keep your pace.

Magnesium glycinate for 50+

Many adults over 50 run low on magnesium. This guide explains why the glycinate form is gentle, how much elemental magnesium you need, how to read the label – 1,440 mg of glycinate is only about 200 mg of magnesium – and which medicines to space apart.

In short
  1. Daily target 420 mg men / 320 mg women, food included
  2. Check the elemental magnesium on the label
  3. Space it 2–4 hours from some medicines

Vitamin B12 for 50+

Up to 1 in 5 adults over 50 run low on B12, often because the stomach absorbs less with age or because of common medicines. Includes the warning signs, which blood tests to ask for and how to read them.

In short
  1. Low B12 can feel like tiredness and brain fog
  2. Ask for active B12 or MMA, not only basic B12
  3. Some common medicines block B12

Vitamin D3 & K2 (MK-7) for 50+

After 50 the skin makes much less vitamin D. D3 helps you absorb calcium, and K2 helps direct it into bones rather than arteries. The guide covers the blood test, sensible daily amounts and an important medication warning.

In short
  1. Take D3 with a meal that contains some fat
  2. Test your vitamin D level first
  3. Blood-thinner users must ask their doctor before K2
