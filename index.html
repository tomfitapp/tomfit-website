Welcome to TOMfit

TOMfit is a simple, private health app for people over 50. Walk, keep your health numbers, keep your list of meds and supplements, and follow easy exercises – all in one place, with large buttons and no sign-up. Your data stays on your phone.

Walk

TOMfit promotes brisk walking – a moderate-intensity form of walking: 100 steps a minute or more, around 4–5 km/h (2.5–3 mph). One big button starts your walk.

  • Start and pause your walk with one button
  • Walking time, brisk minutes, distance, steps and speed
  • Simple daily goals: 30 min, 2 km, 3,000 steps – change them in Settings
  • Brisk walking means 100 or more steps a minute, around 4–5 km/h (2.5–3 mph)
Picture 1 of 1

Brisk walking is a moderate-intensity form of walking. The WHO recommends 150–300 minutes a week. Keep an eye on your pace: 100 or more steps a minute, around 4–5 km/h (2.5–3 mph). TOMfit starts you at the easier end.

Health

Keep your important numbers in one place and see at a glance whether you are going in the right direction.

  • Record your blood glucose
  • Record your blood pressure and pulse
  • Record your weight – BMI is worked out for you
  • Log your fasting time between last and first meal
  • A health report to show your doctor
Picture 1 of 2

I check my blood glucose and blood pressure at least once a day, I keep an eye on my weight and BMI, and I like to check the time between my last and first meal – all to be sure I am going in the right direction.

Medication & supplements

Your full list in one place, with the times you take them and a simple tick when it’s done.

  • Add a medication or supplement: name, dose and form
  • Up to 5 daily times, e.g. breakfast, lunch, bed time
  • Today’s checklist – tap “mark taken”
  • Choose days for items you don’t take every day
  • A printable list for your doctor or pharmacist
Picture 1 of 2

It is not easy to remember all your medications and supplements. Your doctor or pharmacist asks “What do you take?” – and your mind goes blank. I know, because I had that problem too – but not any more. Register all your medications and supplements in the TOMfit app and have the full list in your pocket, ready when you need it.

Exercises

Walking is a great start, but muscles need a little more. TOMfit has 10 easy exercises with large, clear pictures.

  • Daily core: chair squats, calf raises, wall push-ups and more
  • Kettlebell exercises twice a week
  • Count your reps against your own starting point
  • A safety page before you begin
  • Go at your own pace – no levels, no pressure
Picture 1 of 2

The TOMfit app invites you to choose at least 3 very basic daily core exercises – 10 minutes at most – and at least 3 kettlebell exercises, 2–3 times a week. Read the safety notes and tips before you start. Charts show your progress.

Settings

Make TOMfit yours. Keep only the parts you need and set goals that suit you.

  • Switch off the parts you don’t need
  • Set your walking goals
  • Units set automatically – metric or imperial
  • Choose your kettlebell weight
  • All your data stays on your phone – back it up any time
Picture 1 of 1

The TOMfit app is very flexible – it is designed to work for you and with you. Adjust your baseline goals, switch off any part of the app you don’t use, set your kettlebell weight, and more.

Getting started

How do I install TOMfit?

TOMfit is being tested now. Register as a tester (see Test TOMfit on the Home page) and we send you a link to install it from Google Play.

Where is my information stored?

Only on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I use TOMfit without an account?

Yes. There is no sign-up or login.

How do I switch off a part I don’t use?

Settings > page 1 – your app. Switch off walking, health, meds & supps or exercises.

Can I use TOMfit on an iPhone?

TOMfit is made for Android phones.

Walk

How do I start a walk?

On the Walk page, tap the big start button. Tap it again to pause.

How do I change my walking goals?

Settings > page 2 – your goals > adjust your walking goals.

Can I walk with the screen off?

Yes. TOMfit keeps counting your steps.

How do I make distance and speed more exact?

Settings > page 2 – your goals > your step length.

Where do I see my walking progress?

On the Walk page, in the charts for today, this week and the last 12 weeks.

Health

How do I record my blood pressure?

Health page > blood pressure > add new entry.

Where do I see my earlier results?

Health page > recent logs.

Can I record blood sugar in mg/dL?

Yes. Settings > page 1 – your app > blood glucose unit.

How do I log my fasting time?

Health page > fasting time > add new entry, then choose the day on the calendar.

How do I show my results to my doctor?

Use the health report on the Health page.

Meds & Supps and Exercises

How do I add a medication or supplement to my list?

Meds & Supps page > add new entry. Type the name and choose the time.

How do I get a reminder?

Choose a time for your item. TOMfit shows a reminder when it is due.

Can I switch the reminders off?

Yes. Tap “reminder off” on the reminder, or switch them off in Settings.

How do I choose my kettlebell weight?

Settings > page 2 – your goals > adjust your preferred weight.

Where do I find the exercise safety notes?

On the Exercises page, before you start.

Settings and your data

How do I change between km and miles?

Settings > page 1 – your app > units.

How do I make the text bigger?

Settings > page 3 – your data > text size.

How do I back up my data?

Settings > page 3 – your data > backup & restore.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings > page 3 – your data > delete my data.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes, through Google Drive – not directly from phone to phone. On the old phone, save a backup (Settings > page 3 – your data > backup & restore) and keep it on your Google Drive. On the new phone, install TOMfit, then restore the backup from your Google Drive.
