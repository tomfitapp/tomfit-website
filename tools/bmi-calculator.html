What is BMI?

Body Mass Index is a quick way to check if your weight suits your height. It is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres, squared.

BMI = kg ÷ m²
or 703 × lb ÷ in²

A BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 is the healthy range for most adults. BMI does not measure body fat, so strong, muscular people can score high while being lean. Use it as a starting point, together with your waist size.

Over 50? Good to know

For people over 65, research links the lowest health risks with a BMI a little higher, around 23 to 29. Keeping your muscle matters more than chasing a low number.

Quick waist check: your waist should be less than half your height.
