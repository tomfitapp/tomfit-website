Elemental Quiz – the chemical elements

Discover the metals, gases and other elements that make up everything around us.

How to play

You get 15 questions, each harder than the last. Tap a picture to choose it, then press Final answer. A correct answer earns more points each time. Points are safe once you pass question 5 and question 10 – if you go wrong after that, you keep the points from the last safe level.

Stuck? Use each helper once per game: 50 : 50 takes away two wrong answers, Ask the crowd shows how a pretend audience would vote, and Ask a friend gives you a quick opinion. You can also stop at any time and keep your points. After every answer you learn a short fact.
