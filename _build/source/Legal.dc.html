Medical & health disclaimer

Important health notice

1. No medical advice

The TOMfit app is designed solely for motivational, wellness and basic health-tracking purposes for adults aged 50 and over. TOMfit provides only general fitness guidelines and basic health information. TOMfit is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

2. Seek medical guidance

If you are experiencing a medical issue, have health concerns or are seeking medical advice, please contact your doctor (GP) or a qualified healthcare professional as soon as possible. Never delay seeking medical advice, disregard medical advice or stop medical treatment because of information or features in this app.

3. Fitness and exercise safety

The walking goals, basic bodyweight exercises and basic kettlebell strength exercises in TOMfit are intended for general fitness only. If you have a health condition, an injury or any concerns, please check with your doctor that exercising will not put your health at risk. If you feel unwell while exercising, stop straight away and seek medical help and advice.

4. Health-tracking features

Features for recording blood pressure, blood glucose, weight, BMI, fasting times, medication, supplements and health reports for your doctor are strictly for your personal logging convenience. They are not monitored by medical professionals and should not replace clinical medical records or advice.
