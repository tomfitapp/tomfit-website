Sudoku – fill every row, column and box

Tap an empty square, then tap a number. Each number must appear once in every row, column and box.

How to play

Every row, every column and every box must contain each number once. Easy puzzles use a small 6-number grid (numbers 1 to 6, boxes two squares wide and three tall). Medium and hard puzzles use the classic 9-number grid.

Use Notes to jot small pencil marks, Check to see any wrong numbers in red, and Hint to fill in the selected square. Your progress is saved on this device.
