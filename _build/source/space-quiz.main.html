Space Quiz – planets, moons and our Solar System

How to play

You get 20 questions, each harder than the last. Tap a picture to choose it, then press Final answer. A correct answer earns more points each time. Points are safe once you pass question 5, 10 and 15 – if you go wrong after that, you keep the points from the last safe level.

Stuck? Use 50 : 50 once per game to take away two wrong answers. You can also stop at any time and keep your points. After every answer you learn a short fact, and every game uses a fresh mix of questions.
